# Developer Log

Long before code, I built Lego robots and marvelled at Halo’s Spartan armor. That curiosity grew into a fascination with technology that extends what people can do.

I’m Om Sawkare, a final-year student at VIT, working across computer vision, AI workflows, and backend development.

My main focus is Veyra, a website investigation tool I’m building to turn page evidence into clear, useful reports. I want people to understand what needs attention, why it matters, and what the tool could actually verify.

My Adobe hackathon brand-audit project explores how websites communicate with people and AI systems. Four analyzers share a crawl to produce a report with evidence and practical recommendations. Our team reached the top 45 among roughly 100,000 teams.

White Box Detection gave me experience with YOLOv8 and OpenCV; my TigerGraph and FastAPI work strengthened my approach to evidence, data, and backend design.
