# Softer mechanical keyboard sound — 31 October 2026

The oscillator/noise sound was replaced with a real recorded Cherry MX Brown key-down tap. Source: [Keyboard - Press down by Foxfire-](https://freesound.org/people/Foxfire-/sounds/570754/), explicitly licensed CC0 1.0 on its authoritative source page. The public HQ preview supplied by that page was used. No login-only original download was requested. The source describes a press without a release.

The source interval 0.091–0.153 seconds was isolated, high-passed at 160 Hz, low-passed at 4.2 kHz, faded over 0.6 ms at the beginning and 12 ms at the end, and normalized to a sample peak of 0.32. Runtime master gain is 0.16; one voice peaks at no more than 0.0512 (about −25.8 dBFS). Small pitch changes (under 4% including wide keys) and level differences avoid an identical repetitive stamp. Wide keys use the same softened press at a slightly lower pitch/level. No oscillator, reverb, release-clack or long tail was added. Comfortable perceived loudness still depends on the visitor’s speaker volume and needs the user’s audition; no subjective listening approval is claimed by the agent.

One 62 ms, mono, 24 kHz PCM recording is inlined in the client code (2,976 PCM bytes) so the first key needs no network fetch or asynchronous decode. A matching [3,020-byte WAV](previous-soft-tap.wav) and [source/license manifest](../../public/sounds/keyboard/manifest.json) are retained. CC0 permits reuse, modification and redistribution without mandatory credit; the author is credited voluntarily in the code and manifest.

Every trusted physical keydown mapped to the model now triggers one tap, including Shift, Control, Option/Alt, Command/Meta and shortcut letters. Holding a key suppresses browser repeat events; key releases do not double the clicks. No keyboard events are cancelled, synthesized or stored. Hidden-page events and synthetic events stay quiet. The workspace sound switch, saved preference, default-on first-key unlock, eight-voice bound and disposal remain unchanged. Camera, Music, Notes and key animations were not otherwise changed.

Twelve targeted checks passed, including per-key/modifier coverage, held-repeat suppression, hidden/synthetic event filtering, listener/toggle cleanup, lazy unlock, bounded voices, cached buffers, no lingering tail and quiet peak bounds.
