# Touch wallpapers

Coast and Dunes each use an original portrait image generated for this portfolio with OpenAI’s built-in image generator. The portrait masters are 852 × 1847 pixels. The WebP exports retain the original framing. The Settings thumbnails are resized copies of those exports. Landscape uses the existing `coast-light.webp` and `dunes-light.webp` images recorded in the [wallpaper manifest](../manifest.json).

## Portrait prompts

**Coast**

> Create one premium web desktop wallpaper in tall portrait 9:19.5 composition for a phone. Photorealistic Mediterranean limestone coast seen from a high trail, blue-green sea and distant hazy headlands, natural afternoon light, understated refined color and photographic texture. Compose vertically from scratch, with coastline entering diagonally from lower left, a natural bay sitting within the middle 60 percent, muted teal sea and sky providing quiet negative space at the right edge and bottom for app icons. Main landform and distant horizon both inside the central 70 percent of image height, attractive when the top and bottom are trimmed modestly on a portrait tablet. Calm, airy light without blowout. Edge-to-edge photographic image. No text, no logos, no people, no UI, no borders. Produce portrait, not landscape.

**Dunes**

> Create one premium web desktop wallpaper in tall portrait 9:19.5 composition for a phone. Photorealistic soft ochre and cream sculpted desert dunes, subtle dark blue-gray atmospheric sky and gentle late-afternoon light, fine realistic sand detail, restrained earthy palette. Main sinuous wind-sculpted ridge sweeps gently from mid-left down toward center-right, easy to read with icons over it. Keep the key ridge within the central 65 percent of image height so it survives moderate top and bottom trim for portrait tablets. Broad simple shapes with quiet negative space at right edge and bottom, gentle believable shadows, no glowing effects. Edge-to-edge photograph. No text, logos, footprints, people, objects, UI, or borders. Produce portrait, not landscape.
